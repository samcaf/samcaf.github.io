Peer-reviewed papers, my Ph.D. thesis, selected talks, and honors. My name is bolded in each author list. The complete, canonical record lives on INSPIRE-HEP.

Ph.D. Thesis · MIT · 2025

Particles Inside Particles

The Flow of Energy in Quarks, Gluons, and Jets. My doctoral thesis, advised by Jesse Thaler, builds the analytic and probabilistic foundations of energy-correlator observables and the mathematics behind parton-shower simulation — and connects them to real collider data.
